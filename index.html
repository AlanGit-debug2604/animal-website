The Dog

Dogs are loyal, friendly animals that have lived alongside humans for thousands of years. They come in many shapes and sizes, and are known for their intelligence and playful nature.

Because of their strong bond with people, dogs are often considered part of the family.

Interesting Facts

  • Dogs can live in a wide range of habitats, from homes to farms to city apartments.
  • They are omnivores and can eat meat, vegetables, and specially made dog food.
  • Dogs have an excellent sense of smell — up to 100,000 times stronger than humans.
